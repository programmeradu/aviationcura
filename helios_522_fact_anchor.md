Canonical facts for prompt grounding:

- Accident date: 14 August 2005.
- Aircraft: Helios Airways Boeing 737-300, registration 5B-DBY.
- Route: Larnaca, Cyprus to Prague, Czech Republic via Athens, Greece.
- Occupants: 115 passengers and six crew; 121 fatalities.
- Location: terrain near Grammatiko, Greece, approximately 33 km northwest of Athens International Airport.
- Investigation finding: the flight crew became incapacitated by hypoxia after the cabin did not pressurize correctly.
- Key initiating condition: the pressurization mode selector remained in MAN/manual after maintenance rather than AUTO/automatic; the crew did not identify the setting during required checks.
- The cabin-altitude warning was confused with the takeoff-configuration warning because the 737 used the same aural horn for both warnings.
- The aircraft continued under autopilot at high altitude; fuel starvation led to engine flameouts and the final descent.
